Privacy policy

Personal mail tools is a private tool used only by its owner to access their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: read-only access to Gmail and Google Drive, and access to Google Calendar, for the owner's own accounts only.

How data is used: data is read on the owner's own computer to help them find and review their own email, files and calendar. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, except that the owner may ask their AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) to read and summarize items for them.

Storage: access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is stored on any server run by this tool.

Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
